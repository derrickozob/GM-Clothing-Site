Where We Come From

G&M was born from a moment of reflection. The words on our first hoodie weren't ours originally — they came from NBA coach Doc Rivers, spoken during a press conference that stopped a lot of people in their tracks. Our founder was one of them. What started as a single sentence became a brand built to keep that feeling close, and to pass it on.

Named for the People We Love

The name G&M stands for Gold and Molly — the founder's daughter and daughter-in-law, two people who've shaped his life more than almost anything else. Naming the brand after them was a way of tying something personal to something bigger.

G&M symbolizes peace, God, and cultural unity.
